# Bare-Minimum HR Processes Register

**"Stitch-in-time" compounding-liability processes for Australian SMEs**
Australia only. Current as at **August 2026** (thresholds verified against Fair Work / ATO T1 sources, access date **2026-08-10**; key moving figures re-confirmed 2026-08-20).
Maps to: `HQ-AI_AU-HR-Recruitment-Research-Report.md`, `hr_challenges.csv` (PF/PM/RA/RS/X codes), `hr_document_templates.csv` (HR-/RC- doc ids).

---

## The archetype (stay disciplined)

This register catalogues ONE thing: the **stitch-in-time / compounding-liability process** - a low-effort, well-defined, time-sensitive action that is cheap and simple to do correctly at the right moment, but which, if skipped, mistimed, or undocumented, converts into a much larger legal, cost, or time exposure later.

Every entry here has all four defining features:
- **(a) a clock / trigger / window** - there is a date, an event, or a recurring deadline;
- **(b) doing it right is quick and structured** - minutes-to-hours of diarised, templated work;
- **(c) getting it wrong is expensive, risky, or irreversible later** - a quantifiable cost/time/risk delta;
- **(d) SMEs routinely get it wrong** - because no one is tracking the timing.

Anything that is merely "good practice" with no timing trap and no cheap-now/expensive-later delta was **deliberately excluded** (e.g. exit interviews, EVP, generic culture/retention work, most policies that carry no dated trigger).

**Framing - the "minimum viable HR compliance calendar":** each process below is really a reminder waiting to be set. The value an assistant (the People stream) adds is not HR advice - it is *owning the clock*: firing the right prompt, template, or timer at the moment the window opens, so a five-minute action never becomes a five-figure problem.

### PRE-HIRE

#### BMP-009 - Right-to-work / VEVO check before start
- **Bare-minimum action:** Verify work rights via VEVO before commencement; keep a dated record. Make the offer conditional on it.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Before commencement; re-check before any temporary visa expires.
- **Cadence:** Per hire (+ visa-expiry re-check).
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Skipped, assumed, or done after the person starts; no record kept.
- **The hot water later:** Employer sanctions/penalties for employing an unlawful non-citizen. Without a dated due-diligence record there is no defence - the check is cheap; the sanction and reputational hit are not.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Migration Act employer sanctions; VEVO verification (Home Affairs).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** RS14.
- **Linked template id(s):** RC-031 (VEVO check record), RC-038 (conditional offer).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High - routinely skipped or mishandled by SMEs who "trust the CV".
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Pre-start VEVO checklist; visa-expiry re-check timer for temporary-visa holders.
- **Sources:** FWO / Home Affairs VEVO guidance (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-010 - Written contract issued & signed before start
- **Bare-minimum action:** Issue a compliant written contract (award, classification, set-off, hours, employment type) for signature before day 1.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Before commencement.
- **Cadence:** Per hire.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Verbal offers; contract issued after start or never; silent on hours/classification.
- **The hot water later:** Sub-minimum terms are void and replaced by the statutory minimum (back-pay exposure); no valid set-off to rely on when defending award claims; casual/fixed-term status disputes; later "what did we actually agree" disputes with no anchor.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** A contract cannot undercut the NES or applicable award (offending terms void); interplays with s15A (casual definition) and s333E (fixed-term limits).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF1, RS16.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-001/002/003/004 (contracts by type), RC-037 (offer + contract).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High - the FW Act doesn't mandate a written contract for most ongoing staff, so SMEs skip it and lose the set-off and clarity it buys.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Contract-before-start gate; template by employment type with a properly structured set-off clause.
- **Sources:** Report Section 4A #1; FWO contracts guidance (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-011 - Fixed-term 2-year cap + one-renewal + FTCIS
- **Bare-minimum action:** Track fixed-term tenure and renewals; give the Fixed Term Contract Information Statement at each new/renewed contract; stay within the 2-year / one-renewal limits.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** At each offer/renewal; 2-year cumulative cap (rules since 6 Dec 2023).
- **Cadence:** Per fixed-term contract.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Rolling 12-month "trial" contracts; forgetting the FTCIS entirely; not counting back-to-back contracts cumulatively.
- **The hot water later:** The offending term is of no effect and the role can become **permanent by operation of law**; civil penalties for FTCIS non-provision and for deliberate avoidance structuring. A one-page statement forgotten becomes an involuntary permanent hire.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** FW Act s333E-333N; FTCIS obligation.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF2.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-004 (fixed-term contract), HR-009 (FTCIS provide).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High and trending - common in NFP/grant-funded, seasonal, and project SMEs; the exceptions list is narrow and technical.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Fixed-term register with cumulative-tenure + renewal-count flags; FTCIS auto-attach on each contract.
- **Sources:** Report Sections 3.5 & 4A #2 (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-012 - Contractor-vs-employee classification at engagement & on change
- **Bare-minimum action:** Assess the true nature (whole-of-relationship test) at engagement and re-check when the relationship changes; document the reasoning. Note the contractor high-income opt-out ($190,100 from 1 Jul 2026) where relevant.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** At engagement; the test is **dynamic**, not fixed at engagement.
- **Cadence:** On engagement + on any material change.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** "They invoice us, so they're a contractor"; never re-checking as the relationship deepens.
- **The hot water later:** Misclassification exposes unpaid NES, super guarantee charge (**up to 200%**), PAYG, and sham-contracting penalties (up to **$19,800** individual / **$99,000** small business / greater of **$495,000 or 3x** the underpayment for larger businesses).
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** s15AA whole-of-relationship test; s357 sham contracting; s15AB opt-out.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF3, PF4, PF5.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-005 (contractor/services agreement).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High, trending - FWO/ATO coordinated enforcement focus 2025-2026.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Classification questionnaire at engagement; re-assessment prompt on scope change; opt-out-notice flag above $190,100.
- **Sources:** Report Sections 4A #3-5 (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-020 - Defensible shortlisting / interview rationale
- **Bare-minimum action:** Record job-relevant reasons for each shortlist/reject decision and use structured, lawful questions at the time.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** At each screening/interview decision (the record has to be contemporaneous).
- **Cadence:** Per role / candidate.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Gut-feel decisions; no notes; unlawful (protected-attribute) questions; can't reconstruct why later.
- **The hot water later:** A discrimination claim with no contemporaneous, defensible record - the reasoning has to be built after the fact when it is least credible and most contested.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Anti-discrimination law (Cth + state); AHRC.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** RS4, RS7, RS8.
- **Linked template id(s):** RC-017 (shortlisting matrix), RC-016 (CV rubric), RC-023 (unlawful-questions guardrail), RC-024 (interview scorecard).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High - "undocumented, indefensible shortlisting" is a named challenge.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Structured scorecard + reason capture; unlawful-question guardrail surfaced at interview.
- **Sources:** Report Section 5B (T2 practitioner commentary + AHRC T1). Access 2026-08-10.

### DAY 1 / ONBOARDING

#### BMP-002 - Day-1 record-keeping + compliant itemised payslips
- **Bare-minimum action:** Set up 7-year employee records from day 1; issue a compliant, itemised payslip within 1 working day of each pay.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** From day 1; each pay run (payslip within 1 working day of pay day).
- **Cadence:** Every pay cycle; records retained 7 years post-termination.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Manual/informal records; bundling pay into one "gross" figure; missing allowance/penalty line items; gaps in leave/super/classification history.
- **The hot water later:** In any underpayment dispute the **reverse onus of proof shifts to the employer** if records are inadequate - you effectively lose. Civil penalties per contravention accumulate per employee per pay period; itemisation failures are a breach even if the total paid was correct.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** FW Act s535-536 (records) and s536 (payslips); FW Regs 3.31-3.46.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF21, PF22, X2.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-055 (7-yr records), HR-053 (payslip), HR-054 (timesheet).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High / near-universal obligation; high real-world non-compliance among small/informal SMEs.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Auto record-keeping checklist on hire; payslip required-field validator; 7-year retention timer.
- **Sources:** Report Section 4A #21-22; FWO record-keeping fact sheet (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-013 - Fair Work + Casual Employment Information Statements on commencement
- **Bare-minimum action:** Give the FWIS to every new employee (and the CEIS to casuals) on or before commencement.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** On commencement (s125); CEIS also re-issued at 6/12-month intervals.
- **Cadence:** Per hire (+ CEIS intervals).
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Forgotten in onboarding; no record it was provided.
- **The hot water later:** Civil penalty for non-provision; compounds evidentiary weakness in any later dispute (it signals a loose compliance posture). Cheap to attach; awkward to explain the omission later.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** NES s125 (FWIS); CEIS obligation.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF13, PF6.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-007 (FWIS provide), HR-008 (CEIS provide).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High - a pure paperwork step routinely skipped.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Onboarding auto-attach both statements + logged acknowledgement; CEIS interval reminders.
- **Sources:** Report Section 4A #1 / 4B onboarding (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-014 - Superannuation stapled-fund / choice at hire
- **Bare-minimum action:** Give the standard choice form; if no choice is made, request the stapled fund from the ATO before paying to a default fund.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Within 28 days of start; before any default-fund payment.
- **Cadence:** Per hire.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Paying to a default fund without doing the stapled-fund check.
- **The hot water later:** Choice-of-fund shortfall penalties; additional SGC exposure. A two-minute ATO check avoids a penalty on every contribution made to the wrong fund.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Super choice-of-fund + stapled-fund rules (ATO).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF24.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-040 (choice form + stapled-fund note), HR-041 (TFN declaration).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High - a routine onboarding step with a real penalty tail.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Stapled-fund check step in onboarding; 28-day timer.
- **Sources:** ATO stapled-super guidance (T1); report Section 4A #24. Access 2026-08-10.

### EARLY TENURE / PROBATION

#### BMP-001 - Probation review cadence / minimum-employment-period decision
- **Bare-minimum action:** Diarise and run short, documented review meetings with written feedback at set points, and make the keep/exit call before the minimum employment period ends.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Starts on the start date; hard deadline is the minimum-employment-period expiry - **6 months, or 12 months for a small business (<15 employees)**.
- **Cadence:** Reviews at 1 / 3 / 5 months within the window.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** No cadence, no documentation, lose track of the date, "they seemed fine". Also conflating "probation" with the harder-edged jurisdictional minimum-period bar.
- **The hot water later:** Miss the window and the employee gains unfair-dismissal access - termination now needs a valid reason + procedural fairness + likely a PIP; notice can be up to 4-5 weeks instead of 1 (age/tenure dependent); a quick month-5 decision balloons into a months-long, high-risk process. UD compensation cap up to **$95,050** (FY2026-27).
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Unfair dismissal (FW Act Part 3-2); Small Business Fair Dismissal Code; NES notice s117.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM1, PM8.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-065 (probation review/outcome letter), HR-064 (PIP), HR-081 (termination during probation).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Very high - probation mismanagement is a leading driver of avoidable UD claims.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Auto-reminders at 1/3/5 months + a "minimum period ends in 30 days - decide now" alert; supply the review template + feedback structure.
- **Sources:** Report Section 3.7 & 4B (T1 - FWC high-income/comp-cap; verified 2026-08-20). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-005 - Contemporaneous performance documentation + adverse-action timing
- **Bare-minimum action:** File-note every performance/conduct conversation at the time; sequence any adverse action carefully relative to any workplace right the employee has exercised.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** At each incident/conversation; before any adverse action.
- **Cadence:** Continuous.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** No feedback trail; "they knew"; reconstructing notes after the fact; acting right after a complaint or leave/flexibility request.
- **The hot water later:** No documentation means no defensible PIP or dismissal - a direct driver of avoidable unfair-dismissal losses. Ill-timed action after a workplace right triggers **general protections**: reverse onus on the employer, **no compensation cap, and no minimum employment period**. The recurring flashpoint is performance-management timing.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Unfair dismissal (Part 3-2); general protections / adverse action (Part 3-1; s340-361 reverse onus).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM4, PM9.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-069 (informal counselling file note), HR-064 (PIP), HR-062 (review template).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High - "no feedback / no documentation trail" is a named High-prevalence challenge; general protections has no minimum-period and no cap.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Prompt to log after 1:1s; file-note template; an "adverse-action risk-check before you act" gate.
- **Sources:** Report Section 3-watchlist #4 & Section 4B (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-006 - Casual conversion - Employee Choice notification
- **Bare-minimum action:** Track each casual's tenure; when a valid employee-choice notification arrives, respond in writing within 21 days (accept, or refuse on valid grounds).
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Eligibility at **6 months (non-small) / 12 months (small business <15)**; a **21-day** response clock runs from any notification.
- **Cadence:** Per casual, at the 6/12-month mark.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Not tracking tenure; assuming a regular fixed shift pattern is still casual; ignoring, or making a late/invalid, refusal.
- **The hot water later:** Misclassification exposes back-pay of leave and entitlements; a Fair Work Commission dispute; civil penalties for a non-compliant refusal or failure to respond in time.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** s15A casual definition; Employee Choice Pathway; 21-day response obligation.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF6, PF7.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-003 (casual contract), HR-008 (CEIS).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High, trending up - new regime still bedding in; the 12-month small-business wait is a genuine, favourable point of difference SMEs should know.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Tenure tracker with a 6/12-month flag; a 21-day response timer + valid-grounds refusal template.
- **Sources:** Report Sections 3.1(b) & 4A #7 (T1 FWO). Access 2026-08-10.

### ONGOING / COMPLIANCE MAINTENANCE

#### BMP-003 - Annual Wage Review 1 July rate + allowance update
- **Bare-minimum action:** Update base rates and allowances to the new award rates from the first full pay period on/after 1 July.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Each **1 July** (annual).
- **Cadence:** Annual.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Set-and-forget pay rates; missing the 1 July uplift; forgetting allowances also move.
- **The hot water later:** Underpayment accrues from 1 July across the whole workforce; strict-liability back-pay + penalties; compounds silently until an audit or claim.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Award minimum rates (s45 FW Act); Annual Wage Review decision.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF10.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-057 (wage/rate change letter).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High - "keeping up with wage rises" is a High-prevalence foundation challenge; strict liability regardless of intent.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Annual 1 July alert + a new-rate diff per classification.
- **Sources:** Report Section 4A #10; FWO Annual Wage Review (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-004 - Superannuation on-time / Payday Super
- **Bare-minimum action:** Pay SG so it is **received** by the employee's fund within **7 business days** of each payday.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Each payday is day 0; 7-business-day deadline (regime commenced **1 July 2026**). New employees may get a 20-business-day first-contribution window.
- **Cadence:** Every pay cycle.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Still on the quarterly mindset; paying late; treating "sent" as "received"; missing the new-employee 20-day rule.
- **The hot water later:** Super Guarantee Charge applies (non-deductible interest + admin fee) on late/short payments; faster-triggering exposure than the old quarterly system; loss of tax deductibility for late SG.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** SG law; Payday Super (Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025; commenced 1 July 2026). SG rate settled at **12%** since 1 July 2025.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF24, PF25.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-040 (super choice), HR-061 (salary sacrifice).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High - the biggest structural payroll change of 2026; every employer, no size exemption.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Payday-linked super timer; a 7-business-day countdown per pay run; new-employee 20-day-rule flag.
- **Sources:** ATO "About Payday Super" & "Payment deadlines" (T1, verified 2026-08-20); report Section 3.10. Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-007 - Annualised salary annual reconciliation
- **Bare-minimum action:** Record the components the salary covers + outer-limit hours; reconcile actual hours/penalties against the salary each pay period and annually, and top up any shortfall.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Each pay period + an annual reconciliation; award annualised-wage clauses (in force since 1 Mar 2020).
- **Cadence:** Per pay period + annual.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Set-and-forget salary at hire; assuming a "good salary" covers everything; never re-checking against actual rostered hours.
- **The hot water later:** Underpayment even on an above-market salary if actual hours/overtime/penalties in a period exceed the pro-rata salary for that period; Woolworths-style remediation; back-pay + penalties. FWO has flagged this as an ongoing enforcement focus.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Annualised-wage award clauses; s45 FW Act.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF12.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-056 (annualised salary + reconciliation record), HR-012 (IFA - must pass BOOT).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High, trending - arguably the most under-appreciated compliance trap; reconciliation must be per pay period, not an annual average.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Per-pay-period reconciliation calculator + annual reconciliation reminder.
- **Sources:** Report Section 3.6 (Woolworths, 2025) & 4A #12 (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-016 - Flexible-work request: written response within 21 days
- **Bare-minimum action:** Discuss and genuinely try to agree, then respond in writing within 21 days; if refusing, document specific reasonable business grounds and the alternatives considered.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** **21 days** from the written request.
- **Cadence:** Per request.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Informal/verbal refusal; "we're too small to accommodate that" (size is only one factor, not an automatic exemption); no written response inside the window.
- **The hot water later:** The Fair Work Commission can order the request granted or make a different determination if the process was not followed; the refusal is now arbitrable rather than final.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** FW Act s65-65A (flexible work requests; FWC arbitration powers via Secure Jobs, Better Pay).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM12.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-092 (flexible work request response), HR-023 (flexible/hybrid policy).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High, trending - common requests now paired with newer arbitration risk.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** A 21-day response timer on a logged request; reasonable-grounds template + alternatives-considered log.
- **Sources:** FWO flexible-work fact sheet (T1, verified 2026-08-20); report Section 4B challenge 12. Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-015 - Long service leave accrual tracking from start
- **Bare-minimum action:** Record the continuous-service start date and accrue LSL per state/territory rules from day 1.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** From the start date; state qualifying period + pro-rata triggers on termination.
- **Cadence:** Continuous.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** No tracking; start date lost; ignoring pro-rata entitlement on early termination.
- **The hot water later:** Back-pay of the LSL entitlement + state penalties; pro-rata payout owed on termination (e.g. NSW after 5 years for most reasons) miscalculated or missed. Entitlements differ sharply by state (e.g. NSW/QLD/WA/TAS 8.6667 wks at 10 yrs; VIC/ACT 6.0667 wks at 7 yrs; SA/NT 13 wks at 10 yrs).
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** State/territory LSL Acts (NSW LSL Act 1955; VIC LSL Act 2018; QLD IR Act 2016) - LSL sits outside the NES.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PF17.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-050 (LSL application & calculation record).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High - accrual is invisible until an exit or milestone, so it is rarely tracked from the start when it is cheap.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** A service-date clock per employee; a state-rule engine; a pro-rata alert on exit.
- **Sources:** Report Section 4A #17 (T1 state Acts). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-017 - Positive duty (sexual harassment) prevention
- **Bare-minimum action:** Maintain a policy + training + reporting pathway proactively (not just react to complaints) - the AHRC 7 Standards scaled to business size.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Ongoing proactive duty (positive duty since Dec 2022; AHRC enforcement powers since 12 Dec 2023).
- **Cadence:** Periodic policy + training refresh.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Reactive "we'll deal with it if someone complains"; no policy or training record.
- **The hot water later:** AHRC inquiries, compliance notices, court-ordered compliance and enforceable undertakings; vicarious liability for harassment that occurs. **No size threshold** - a 3-person business has the same duty, scaled to what is reasonable.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Sex Discrimination Act positive duty (Respect@Work); AHRC monitoring/enforcement.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM20.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-016 (bullying/discrimination/SH policy), HR-094 (reasonable adjustment), HR-017 (EEO policy).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High, trending - still widely misread as a large-employer issue.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Policy + training-refresh cadence reminders; a 7-Standards self-check.
- **Sources:** Report Section 3.8 (T1 AHRC). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-018 - WHS psychosocial hazard risk-assessment cadence
- **Bare-minimum action:** Identify-assess-control-review psychosocial hazards with a documented register; consult workers; review periodically and after incidents.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Ongoing duty; review on change or incident.
- **Cadence:** Periodic review.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** No documented process; treating it as an EAP/wellbeing nice-to-have; no worker consultation.
- **The hot water later:** WHS penalties (severe; officer imprisonment for reckless conduct in most jurisdictions); regulator improvement/prohibition notices. **No small-business size carve-out** (unlike the FW Act's <15 threshold) - applies to single-director companies and sole traders with even one worker.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** WHS Act primary duty of care; Managing Psychosocial Hazards Codes of Practice (in force in every jurisdiction; VIC newest, 1 Dec 2025).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM14, PM15.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-096 (psychosocial hazard register), HR-015 (WHS policy), HR-097 (WHS risk assessment/SWMS).
- **Prevalence / why common:** High, trending sharp - now a mainstream WHS duty widely misread as optional.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Psychosocial hazard register template; periodic review reminder; incident-triggered reassessment.
- **Sources:** Report Section 3.4 (T1 WHS regulators/Codes). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-021 - Privacy / automated-decision-making transparency update (from 10 Dec 2026)
- **Bare-minimum action:** Update the privacy collection notice to disclose automated decision-making used in hiring before the commencement date.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Hard date - **10 December 2026**.
- **Cadence:** One-off compliance update (then maintained).
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Not tracking the commencement date; no ADM disclosure in the privacy/collection notice.
- **The hot water later:** OAIC enforcement under the amended Privacy Act; separate discrimination exposure if AI screening produces discriminatory outcomes. A forward-dated, cheap policy edit now vs a compliance gap the moment the rule switches on.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** Privacy Act automated-decision-making transparency (commences 10 Dec 2026); general anti-discrimination law already applies to AI-assisted hiring (no AI carve-out).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** RS22, RS23.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-019 (privacy/data collection notice), RC-015 (candidate privacy notice/consent).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med, trending sharp - live obligation with a fixed near-term date many SMEs will miss.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Countdown to 10 Dec 2026; a privacy-notice ADM-disclosure clause template.
- **Sources:** Report Section 5B (AI-in-hiring) (T1 OAIC/legislation + T2 commentary). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-008 - Redundancy consultation before acting
- **Bare-minimum action:** Complete award/agreement consultation and genuinely consider redeployment **before** the decision is finalised or implemented.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** Before the decision is implemented; the award consultation-clause obligation triggers on a definite decision to make a major change.
- **Cadence:** One-off per restructure.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Deciding first and "consulting" after; skipping consultation; using "redundancy" to remove an underperformer.
- **The hot water later:** Even a genuine redundancy can be an **unfair dismissal** if consultation/redeployment was skipped; back-pay if award/contract redundancy pay was owed (the small-business exemption covers redundancy *pay*, not process or notice); a role reappearing shortly after is a red flag that unwinds the "genuine" defence.
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** NES redundancy (s119-121); modern-award consultation clause; genuine-redundancy test (s389).
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM10.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-086 (consultation letter), HR-087 (selection criteria matrix), HR-091 (major-change consultation record), HR-089 (redeployment offer).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High - owners hear "small business = no redundancy pay" and stop there, missing the consultation/redeployment obligations that still bind.
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** A "consult before you decide" gate; consultation letter + timeline; redeployment checklist.
- **Sources:** Report Section 4B challenge 10 (T1). Access 2026-08-10.

#### BMP-019 - Termination notice + correct final pay
- **Bare-minimum action:** Apply the correct NES (or award/contract, whichever is greater) notice scale by tenure; pay all accrued entitlements; meet state final-pay timing.
- **Clock / trigger / window:** At termination.
- **Cadence:** Per exit.
- **What SMEs get wrong:** Flat notice regardless of tenure; forgetting the extra week for over-45s with 2+ years' service; unpaid accrued leave; assuming the small-business redundancy-pay exemption also removes notice.
- **The hot water later:** An underpayment dispute layered on top of any dismissal claim; **1 week vs up to 4-5 weeks'** notice depending on tenure/age; FWO recovery + superannuation on top. Notice still applies to small business (only redundancy *pay* is exempted).
- **Fair Work / legal exposure:** NES notice of termination (s117); correct final pay; state final-pay timing.
- **Linked challenge_code(s):** PM11.
- **Linked template id(s):** HR-082 (notice + final pay statement), HR-100 (resignation acknowledgement), HR-102 (property/access checklist).
- **Prevalence / why common:** Med-High - persistently mishandled due to lack of systemisation (manual/by-memory calculation).
- **Proactive-assistant hook:** Tenure-based notice calculator; final-pay checklist incl. accruals + state timing; LSL pro-rata cross-check (BMP-015).
- **Sources:** Report Section 4B challenge 11 (T1 FWO; FW Act s117). Access 2026-08-10.
