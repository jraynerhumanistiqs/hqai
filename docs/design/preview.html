HQ.ai design tokens

Light surfaces: the unscoped :root fallback and [data-app="product"]. The dark surfaces - marketing and product dark - are in preview-dark.html.

HQ.ai has no single light/dark pair. It has two surface palettes selected by data-app on <html>, and only the product one has a light/dark toggle. Marketing is always dark. This sheet is therefore split by lightness, not by theme. Values transcribed from app/globals.css; if they disagree, globals.css is right and this file is stale.

See DESIGN.md for roles, traps and component recipes.
