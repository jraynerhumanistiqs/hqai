danger and warning, against their own tints

Both render as bg-x/10 text-x pills. A tint of the same hue pulls the background toward the text, so the tinted pair always reads lower than the bare surface does. Every pill below is the real tinted composite on the real surface, at the alphas the codebase actually uses.

Each pill is labelled with its worst tinted ratio across page / card / inset. The strips at the bottom of each surface put gold, warning and danger together as solid fills, because all three are warm and keeping them apart is the real constraint here - not just clearing 4.5:1.
